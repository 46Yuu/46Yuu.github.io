Description

Be a Shoe is a Fortnite game where the player takes on the role of a shoe and grows larger by eating food. More than 100 skins are available to unlock, each with 3 VFX.

Developed as part of a team of 4.

  • Creation and design of the ShoeDex page, equipment system, and crafting system.
  • Added SFX and one of the trails unlockable through crafting.
