Description

Mission Maintenance is a tablet game, playable offline, designed to showcase the M2E maintenance professions to potential candidates in approximately 2 minutes during RATP recruitment fairs.

Initially developed by a team of 10 people, then finalized by a team of 5.

Project duration: 7 months.

My Contributions

  • Developed and modified multiple mini-games.
  • Debugged and resolved gameplay issues across various systems.
  • Implemented data binding between components.
  • Design and implementation of the mini-game selection system in the options menu.
  • Creation of a player assistance system for error messages and guidance.
