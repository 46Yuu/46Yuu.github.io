Description

Depths of Hue is a narrative puzzle game set underwater, where the player must recover their memories by exploring an underwater temple.

Developed as part of a team of thirteen.

5 developers, 2 artists, and 6 business team members.

Project duration: 2 months.

My Contributions

  • Designed and developed the Sudoku-inspired mini-game.
  • Created the lost map mini-game, where the player must find the correct order of locations.
  • Implemented fade transitions between scenes.
  • Implemented dialogue systems and French/English localization.
  • Developed the audio manager system.
  • Created the game's menus and options system.
  • Implemented the hint system.
