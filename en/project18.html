Description

Canyon Carnage is a fast-paced and intense FPS where the player must escape a mysterious canyon infested with monsters. To succeed, the player must survive by defeating enemies and solving puzzles in ancient temples to progress through the canyon.

Developed as part of a team of 4.

Project duration: 8 months.

My Contributions

  • Designed the game's core gameplay and mechanics.
  • Designed the temple levels and implemented them into the game.
  • Designed and animated the player's combo UI.
  • Created the portal visual effects (VFX).
  • Designed and implemented the first temple puzzle.
